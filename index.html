— METHODOLOGY

The Architecture of Conception

Our protocol is defined by iterative refinement—progressing from raw site listening to digital millimeter twins, followed by bespoke hand craftsmanship.

“We do not design buildings to impress the camera; we construct geometries that elevate the human nervous system.”

— KAELEN VANCE, FOUNDING PRINCIPAL
PHASE 01

Site Forensic & Topological Resonance

Comprehensive lidar scanning, subterranean hydrology mapping, and seasonal light angle solar audits to understand the site's primordial narrative.

PHASE 02

Spatial Geometry & Monolithic Massing

Sculpting volumetric voids and solid masses. We balance sightlines with sacred privacy corridors, crafting intuitive domestic choreographies.

PHASE 03

Material Rigor & Digital Twin BIM

Quarry visits in Carrara and Swiss Graubünden to hand-select blocks of stone. Detailed Level 400 BIM modeling for micron-tight structural tolerances.

PHASE 04

Artisanal Fabrication & Execution

Daily site presence under partner supervision. Custom millwork, flush architectural glazing, and bespoke landscape integration to final turnkey delivery.

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Commissioning a bespoke architectural work is an intimate, high-stakes journey. Here are concise answers to common client questions regarding timelines, investment structures, and site parameters.

Ask A Specific Question

What is the standard timeline for a bespoke luxury residence?

A complete custom estate typically requires 6 to 9 months for schematic design, engineering, and municipal approvals, followed by 12 to 18 months for precision construction and interior detailing.

Do you take on architectural commissions internationally?

Yes. Architecture Prime maintains active projects across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and East Asia. We partner with vetted local structural engineers to satisfy regional municipal requirements.

How do you ensure budget control during turnkey construction?

We operate with transparent Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) contracts, detailed material schedules, and rigorous independent quantity surveying at every construction milestone.

Can you incorporate passive house & off-grid solar technology?

Absolutely. Over 60% of our residential commissions are certified LEED Platinum or Net-Zero Ready, integrating concealed solar arrays, geothermal heating, and rainwater reclamation.

How does your client portal work for remote patrons?

Through our encrypted Client Portal, patrons receive weekly 4K drone videography, live site camera access, financial disbursement ledgers, and interactive 3D BIM model updates in real time.
