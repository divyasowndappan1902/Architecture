Company Introduction

An International Atelier of Radical Refinement

Architecture Prime was founded as an antidote to disposable mass construction. We operate at the intersection of monolithic structural clarity and poetic human sensory experience.

Headquartered with ateliers in Zurich and New York, our practice brings together master architects, parametric engineers, interior curators, and environmental physicists to manifest residences that stand untouched by fleeting fashion.

"Architecture must possess gravity without arrogance; it must ground the dweller while elevating their gaze."

The Journey

From Milanese Classical Roots to Global Modernism

The atelier began with a single hillside residence in Lake Como. That initial project crystallized our signature design language: sunken concrete frames, vast apertures of shadowless glass, and unadorned volcanic stone.

Over eighteen years, Architecture Prime expanded into bespoke estates across the Swiss Alps, oceanfront pavilions in Southern California, and corporate cultural monuments in Tokyo. Yet our founding rigor remains intact: we accept only twelve custom commissions per year to guarantee total principal involvement.

Spatial Doctrine

The Pillars of Architecture Prime

Every line drawn in our studios adheres to three non-negotiable architectural tenets.

01. Contextual Harmony

A building must appear as if it grew organically from its native topography. We study natural sun angles, wind corridors, and ancestral geological lines to anchor every design in its place.

02. Honest Materiality

We reject faux finishes and superficial veneers. Our palettes celebrate the raw tactile beauty of hand-poured board-formed concrete, bush-hammered granite, oxidized bronze, and solid white oak.

03. Light As Matter

Daylight is not treated as mere illumination; it is our primary building material. We sculpt deep shadow-reveals and skylight light-wells that mark the poetic passage of time across bare walls.

The Stackly Chronology

Milestones of our architectural pilgrimage across four continents.

2008

Zurich Foundation

Kaelen Vance and Hélène Marceau establish Stackly Atelier along the Limmat River in Zurich, completing their first alpine retreat in St. Moritz.

2012

Venice Architecture Biennale

Commissioned to erect the Swiss Pavilion installation “Tectonic Stillness,” exploring monolithic cast concrete volumes interacting with tidal lagoon light.

2016

Tokyo Studio Opening (Aoyama)

Expanding into East Asia with our Aoyama studio, partnering with master carpenter guilds in Kyoto to merge traditional joinery with parametric carbon concrete.

2024 – 2026

New York Flagship Atelier & Global Honors

Establishing our Americas headquarters on Madison Avenue, delivering landmark carbon-neutral residential towers and winning global architectural accolades.
